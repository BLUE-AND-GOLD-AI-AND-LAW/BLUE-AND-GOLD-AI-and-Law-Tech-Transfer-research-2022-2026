GITHUB REPOSITORY. 

BLUE AND GOLD AI AND LAW-TECH TRANSFER RESEARCH 2022-2026.

Website address: https://github.com/BLUE-AND-GOLD-AI-AND-LAW/BLUE-AND-GOLD-AI-and-Law-Tech-Transfer-research-2022-2026.

BLUE AND GOLD AI and Law-Tech Transfer research 2022-2026 is a public repository of Public Information gathered by article 6 Mexico's Constitution proceedings before INAI and Transparency for the People authorities, between 2022 and 2026.

The order of the information is as follows:
1.	1.	ZIP Files from Technology Development records, from The Big Five federal universities in Mexico (UNAM, IPN, CINVESTAV, UAM, TECNM), and the 32 State Universities (Part I and Part II). This repository includes the whole structure of our empirical approach during 2014-2022 period, supported at some degree by PDF file from a consolidated information of the 5 Big Federal Universities in Mexico, The Big Five, (2022).
2.	Excel files of summary of patents, copyrights, and labor relations schemes. These records were analyzed from Mexican Intellectual Property Institute Platforms during 2014-2022 period, including MARCIA and SIGA, as well as the process to share the whole records from Public Universities of Mexico in 2023, to reach the whole information regarding intellectual property rights and labor relations schemes.
3.	Public information from Mexico's Senate and Mexico's Congress regarding AI regulation projects (2024). The following repository stands about the core process of AI Regulatory research, formal events and Open Discussions from Senate of Mexico and Mexican House of Representatives, with the support of both Institutions Research Policymaking.
4.	Public information from CONAHCYT-SEHICTI (2024), case RRA 10604/24 before INAI (September 2024). This legal case shows the whole National System of Researchers database (SNII) access by inventions and tech-transfer proceedings, including RIZOMA platform.
5.	Public Information related to Legal Informatics, AI and Law and Legal Education Courses in Public Universities of Mexico (2026). The repository includes 35 Public Universities requests to overlook the state of Legal Informatics in Mexico into legal planning of education and pedagogy. 
6.	TFCA Court Statistics Report (March 1-31, 2024). The following public statistics records is available to share the whole picture in bureaucratic proceedings.
7.	APPENDIX A, legal case titled “Overcoming the generational gap”. An academic legal case to overlook into legal planning the AI and Law modern subject and policymaking process.
8.	APPENDIX B Database of AI and Law Legal Informatics 2026 PNT petitions (Part 1 to Part 7, and Excel File consolidated).  You can verify these academic facts in 34 Public Universities with the PNT proceeding, the curriculum design that it currently holds, and if there are lecturers or Academic Group for the subject.
9.	APPENDIX C Database of Tech-Transfer 2022-2023 PNT petitions to state universities. (2023).
10.	APPENDIX D Database of Professors Positions and paid jobs in University of Veracruz (UV), PNT petitions (2024). The records from The University of Veracruz Public Contests of Teaching Positions during 2014-2024 period, and the proceedings IVAI-REV/1378/2024/II and IVAI-REV/1579/2024/I, petitions 300564224000371 and 300564224000510 demonstrates our approach.
11.	APPENDIX E Letter to Mexican Senate (AI Committee), dated Feb 6th, 2026.
12.	Mexican AI Act draft (April 20th, 2026). 

The public repository linked in APPENDIX B, APPENDIX C, and APPENDIX D, database records (2022-2026), and the Cases proceedings before INAI, Transparency for the People and IVAI can also be consulted in the National Transparency Platform in https://www.plataformadetransparencia.org.mx/.
